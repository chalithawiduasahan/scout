Cold outreach takes longer than the automation itself

Finding a lead is one task. Researching what they'd actually need is another. Building a convincing sample of it, by hand, before you even know if they'll reply — that's most of an automation freelancer's week, and none of it scales. Scout does the research, builds a real working demo, and drafts the pitch — so the only manual step left is deciding whether to hit send.
